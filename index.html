    HOW THIS TREE WORKS

    Reading the tree

    The tree grows the way a real one does: the oldest generation is at the bottom, and each generation grows one level higher. The trunk is Vincent and Rosaria, Alfred's parents in Torricella Sicura, Italy.

    Every fork in a branch is a couple. Each partner sits on their own twig, and their children's branches grow up out of that fork.

    Ink branch: a confirmed link. Pencil dashes: we think so, but it still needs confirming. Empty bud: someone we know belongs here but haven't added yet. Polaroid: a photo, or initials until we have one.

    Getting around

    • Move: drag the page. On a phone, drag with one finger.
    • Zoom: scroll, pinch, or use the + and − buttons. Fit shows the whole tree again.
    • Meet someone: tap their name. Their card shows dates, a short story, and their parents, partners and children. Tap any of those names to jump to that person.
    • Photos: if someone has photos in the family album, they're in their card. Tap one to see it larger, and use the arrows (or swipe) to see the rest.
    • Find someone: type a name in the search box and press Enter. If they're inside a folded branch, it opens for you.
    • Follow a line: when you tap someone, everyone outside their line (parents, grandparents, partner, children and grandchildren) fades back so their branch stands out.
    • Fold a branch: tap the small − pill under any couple to tuck their descendants away. It turns into + and a count; tap it again to open that family one generation at a time. Very big families open with the younger branches folded.
    • Collapse everything: the button under Fit folds every branch back to the first families in one tap. Tap it again to open them all.
    • Zoomed out: names give way to family labels. Tap a label to open that family: you'll see their children, and each child's family stays folded until you tap it too. The GEN markers on the left show which generation you're looking at, and the small map in the corner shows where you are.
    • Save as image: downloads a picture of the tree as it's showing now (folded branches stay folded), ready for the family group chat.

    Adding to the tree

    This is the part we need you for. look after this tree together, and Cristobal enters every update on the site by hand, so it stays tidy and consistent. You don't need an account, and there's nothing here for you to edit.

    News travels by message. Message a keeper whatever you know about someone:

    • their full name, and what people call them
    • how they fit in (whose child, partner or parent they are)
    • birth and death years, if you know them
    • a sentence or two of story: where they lived, what they did, something people remember
    • corrections count too: a wrong date or a misspelled name is worth a message

    Who gets a card? Husbands, wives and partners do, when they're a parent of someone on the tree or a partner in the main family: they're the other half of the fork the children grow from. A former partner with no children on the tree is usually a line in someone's story instead of a card of their own, and partners of anyone whose own place is still in pencil wait until that branch is confirmed.

    Photos, documents and videos go in the family folder. The D'Alessio Family Archive is our shared Google Drive folder; the next section explains how to get in and how to use it. No access yet? Send the file with your message instead, and we'll put it in.

    Don't know everything? Send it anyway. Blanks are fine, and anything we're unsure about goes in pencil until someone confirms it. Updates go in in batches, so yours may take a little while to appear.

    The family folder

    The D'Alessio Family Archive is a shared Google Drive folder where the family's photos, documents and home videos live. It's how pictures and clips reach this tree.

    Getting in. The archive is invite-only. Ask and one of them will add you; Julie helps look after the archive. You'll need a Google account (a free Gmail address works). Google will email you a link, and afterwards the archive also sits in your Drive under "Shared with me".

    Adding your files. There's a folder for every person on this tree. Open the folder of the person your photo or video is mostly about and add the file there. A photo with several family members in it can go in each of their folders, or just the main person's: close enough is genuinely fine. Can't decide? Put it in Unsorted and we'll file it. Phone photos and videos are welcome exactly as they are; we'll pick the best ones for the site.

    Two house rules: everyone in the family who joins the archive can see everything in it, so if something should stay private, send it to Cristobal or Julie directly instead. And please don't move or delete anyone else's files.
