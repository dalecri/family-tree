D'ALESSIO ORIGINS

Where the name comes from, and what the records say

The D'Alessio name is Italian, and the branch on this tree has been in Cornwall, Ontario since at least the early 1900s. This page gathers what we've found so far about Alfred and Simone, the other D'Alessio families in Cornwall, and where in Italy the name likely started. Some of it is confirmed, and some of it is still a good guess. We've tried to say which is which.

The name in Italy today

D'Alessio means "of Alessio" (Italian for Alexis or Alexander). The surname is found mostly in central and southern Italy.

Three D'Alessio lines in Cornwall

At least three D'Alessio families were in Cornwall by the mid-1900s. No record found so far shows how, or whether, they're related.

How Italians came to Cornwall

  • Cornwall's Italian community began arriving in the early 1900s.
  • Most came by rail from Halifax and found work on the railways, the canal and in construction.
  • Families usually followed a first "pioneer" from the same Italian town, then sent for relatives and neighbours. This is called chain migration.

Wikipédia (French); Canadian Museum of Immigration at Pier 21

Clues to where in Italy

1900

Alfred's birth year

Alfred was born in 1900, which places the family's arrival in Canada in the late 1890s or early 1900s, right in the first wave of Italian immigration to Cornwall.

V.

His middle initial

Alfred's middle initial V may stand for Vincent or Vincenzo. A middle name like that often comes from a grandfather. Alfred and Simone also named one of their sons Vincent.

S

Southern Italian in-laws

Desimone and Giannetti, the Italian surnames that married into the Cornwall D'Alessio families, are both southern Italian names. That fits a chain migration from one district in southern Italy.

A

An Abruzzo family

Another D'Alessio family, Elio D'Alessio (son of Giovanni D'Alessio and Anna Rabottini), came through Pier 21 from Chieti Scalo in Abruzzo. Abruzzese D'Alessios settled in the region too, but no link to the Cornwall families has been found.
